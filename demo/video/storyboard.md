# Storyboard — Encore, 60 s vertical short

Compressed from `demo/demo_script.md` (7 beats) into 8 scenes / 60 s. Punchlines in English
(matching the pitch), VO can be French. Design rules: `frame.md`. Composition: `index.html`.

Positioning to land: **"Shazam identifies tracks. Encore captures nights — in real time,
without the cloud."**

## Shot notes

- Scenes 3 and 6 use the HTML replicas of the app screens (timeline + Capture Vibe card);
  scenes 2 and 8 use the brand layer (see `frame.md`) — green for the app, pink for the
  brand, never mixed.
- Scene 4 is the emotional core (beat 3 of the live demo script) — give it the longest hold
  and the only red in the whole video.
- Keep every scene readable with the sound off: one punchline at a time, never two text
  blocks on screen together.
