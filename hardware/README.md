# hardware/ — case, wiring, power

[← Main README](../README.md) · Firmware pins: [firmware/](../firmware/README.md)

The physical pendant: a **3D rounded heart** — clean, minimalist, emoji-like silhouette with softly rounded edges — made of **two hollow snap-fit shells** (front + back, not a lid) around the XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense and its LiPo, with a **raised pulse-wave relief** centered on the front. It hangs from a chain or ring through an integrated Ø5.5 mm hole at the top cleft. Geometry only at this stage — no texture or color.

**Dimensions: 55.5 × 58.1 × 18.4 mm** (+1 mm relief) · bail hole Ø5.5 · pocket height 14.0 mm vs 13.6 mm component stack.

## Design (generated by [`3dprint/pendant.py`](3dprint/pendant.py))

- **Outer solid**: a loft of the implicit heart curve, inset per-depth by an edge-rounding (r 4.2) + gentle doming profile — no sharp edges anywhere; **clean visible seam at mid-depth** where the shells meet.
- **`encore_back.stl`** — back shell: fitted component pocket, **continuous snap lip with a 0.3 mm ridge** around the inner perimeter, half of the bail.
- **`encore_front.stl`** — front shell: **raised pulse relief** (1 mm, chamfered base), matching **snap channel + groove** (the discreet closure — press to click, pry at the seam to open), **USB-C slot** in the right wall just above the seam (charge/flash without opening), **3 mic holes** over the Sense PDM mic, half of the bail.
- **Fitted interior**: the pocket is the smoothed outline of (battery ∪ board ∪ wire bay) + 0.6 mm — the components fit it exactly, nothing rattles.
- **Exact component models** (in the views + viewer): the XIAO ESP32-S3 **Sense** without camera — base PCB 21 × 17.8 × 1.1 with castellated pad rows, RF shield, USB-C 8.94 × 3.26 overhanging the right edge, reset/boot buttons, U.FL antenna connector, B2B standoffs, Sense PCB with **empty camera socket, microSD slot and PDM mic** (total stack 7.5 mm — this is why the case is 18.4 deep, the flat 3.4 mm assumption was wrong); the **LP502030** LiPo (20.5 × 32 × 5.3, PCM under the Kapton band at the wire end, red/black AWG26 leads, JST-PHR-02 folded flat in the wire bay).
- **Rule (CLAUDE.md): never edit the STLs — edit `pendant.py` and regenerate.** Run from inside `3dprint/`. Needs `pip install trimesh shapely manifold3d numpy mapbox_earcut`. The generator **auto-sizes the heart** and auto-places the stack — change a component constant and everything re-fits.

## Views & interactive model

- `python pendant.py --render` → **`four_views.png`**: 1 closed front · 1b side (thickness + seam) · 2 open shells + components · 3 profile + dimensions.
- `python pendant.py --viewer` → **`viewer.html`**: self-contained interactive 3D (no network needed) — drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, slider/button to **open and assemble** the two shells with the battery, board and USB modeled at true dimensions. `viewer.html?explode=0` opens it exploded.

## Component layout (baked into the generator)

**Stacked**: LiPo flat at the back of the pocket, XIAO on foam tape on top, Sense board toward the front, **USB-C edge against the right wall** (1 mm from the slot). Battery wires exit left into the wire bay (sized for the folded JST — which you cut anyway at assembly). The flexible rod antenna patch sticks flat to the inside of the front shell next to the camera socket.

**The header pins ("feet")**: breadboard headers, not fixings — **clip them flush** (the pocket assumes it); they'd otherwise press into the battery pouch. Solder the three signal wires (touch → GPIO1, LED → GPIO2, 3V3/GND) into the through-holes from the top side. **The camera module unplugs** (lift the FPC latch); only the mic is used.

## Assembly

1. Battery into the back-shell pocket, wires left. No JST socket on the XIAO — cut the connector, solder leads to the **BAT+/BAT− pads** (mind polarity; on-board circuit charges via USB-C).
2. Foam tape, XIAO on top, USB-C toward the right wall.
3. WS2812B (3V3/GND/DIN→GPIO2) + copper-tape touch pad (GPIO1) inside the front shell, per [`wiring.md`](wiring.md).
4. Align the front shell (USB slot over the connector), **press until the perimeter ridge clicks**. Open by prying gently at the seam near the tip. Chain or split ring through the bail.

## Print notes (42 fablab / LabElec)

FDM **or resin**. FDM: print **both shells seam-face down** (the flat side on the bed) — the rounded outer faces then need no supports; allow light internal supports over the pocket ceiling of the front shell (hidden inside). 0.2 mm layers, 2–3 perimeters. Resin: hollow shells print as-is. **Print a test fit first** and tune `SLIP` / `RIDGE` in `pendant.py` — snap-fit tolerance is printer-dependent.
