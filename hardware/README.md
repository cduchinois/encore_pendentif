# hardware/ — case, wiring, power

[← Main README](../README.md) · Firmware pins: [firmware/](../firmware/README.md)

The physical pendant: a **3D rounded heart** — clean, minimalist, emoji-like silhouette with softly rounded edges — made of **two hollow snap-fit shells** (front + back, not a lid) around the XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense and its LiPo, with the **pulse waveform engraved** across the front face. It hangs from a chain or ring through an integrated Ø5.5 mm hole at the top cleft. Geometry only at this stage — no texture or color.

**Dimensions: 55.5 × 58.1 × 18.4 mm** (pulse engraved 1.2 mm) · bail hole Ø5.5 · pocket height 14.0 mm vs 13.6 mm component stack.

## Design (generated by [`3dprint/pendant.py`](3dprint/pendant.py))

- **Outer solid**: a loft of the implicit heart curve, inset per-depth by an edge-rounding (r 4.2) + gentle doming profile — no sharp edges anywhere; **clean visible seam at mid-depth** where the shells meet.
- **`encore_back.stl`** — back shell: hollow interior, **continuous snap lip with a 0.3 mm ridge** around the inner perimeter, half of the bail.
- **`encore_front.stl`** — front shell: **engraved pulse** (1.2 mm, prints crisp against the bed), matching **snap channel + groove** (the discreet closure — press to click, pry at the seam to open), **USB-C slot** in the right wall just above the seam (charge/flash without opening), **5 mic holes + an internal acoustic chamber** over the Sense PDM mic, a **capacitive touch zone behind the pulse** (the engraving thins the wall to ~1.0 mm — the pulse itself is the pin button, copper tape glued flat behind it), the **FPC WiFi antenna glued under the ceiling's upper band** (away from the copper, the mic chamber and the battery; U.FL pigtail in a loose loop), half of the bail.
- **Interior**: `POCKET = "hollow"` (the default, print this first) leaves the full heart interior open — maximum slack while the real components are unverified; fix the stack with foam tape / hot glue. Switch to `"fitted"` after measuring the real parts for an exact pocket where nothing rattles.
- **Mic acoustics**: the exact mic XY on the Sense board is **unverified** (docs unreachable from the build sandbox; it is on the top face next to the camera connector). Mitigation built in: a 16 × 12 × 1.4 mm **acoustic chamber** recessed into the front shell spans the whole plausible zone, vented by the 5-hole cluster — a few mm of mic offset cannot break the sound path. **When the board arrives: find the tiny mic port hole, measure its XY from the board corner, set `MIC_POS` in `pendant.py`, regenerate.**
- **Exact component models** (in the views + viewer): the XIAO ESP32-S3 **Sense** without camera — base PCB 21 × 17.8 × 1.1 with castellated pad rows, RF shield, USB-C 8.94 × 3.26 overhanging the right edge, reset/boot buttons, U.FL antenna connector, B2B standoffs, Sense PCB with **empty camera socket, microSD slot and PDM mic** (total stack 7.5 mm — this is why the case is 18.4 deep, the flat 3.4 mm assumption was wrong); the **LP502030** LiPo (20.5 × 32 × 5.3, PCM under the Kapton band at the wire end, red/black AWG26 leads, JST-PHR-02 folded flat in the wire bay).
- **Rule (CLAUDE.md): never edit the STLs — edit `pendant.py` and regenerate.** Run from inside `3dprint/`. Needs `pip install trimesh shapely manifold3d numpy mapbox_earcut`. The generator **auto-sizes the heart** and auto-places the stack — change a component constant and everything re-fits.

## Views & interactive model

- **Chain (chosen): third-party Cuban/curb chain, 64 cm** — chunky print-in-place curb links (46 links, 24.8 × 20 × 6.4 mm each, no supports, no clasp, slips over the head), far more robust than thin printed rings. **The bail hole was enlarged to Ø9.8 (disc Ø16) so its ~6.5 × 6.4 mm link wire threads through.** The STL is third-party (downloaded model): place it at `3dprint/third_party/cuban_chain_64cm.stl` (gitignored — not redistributed in this repo); `--viewer` embeds it automatically when present, with the ⛓ chain toggle (`?chain=1`).
- **Chain clip (third-party, scaled ×0.78)** — a two-half snap-link that connects a chain link to the bail: original wire is 6 × 10 mm (diagonal 11.7 — too big for the Ø9.8 bail), so the generator exports a **×0.78 copy** (`third_party/encore_clip_scaled78.stl`, wire 4.7 × 7.8, diagonal 9.1) that threads both the bail and the cuban link opening (~12.8 × 8). Place the original at `3dprint/third_party/chainclip.stl` (gitignored); `--viewer` then embeds the assembled clip between bail and bottom link (⛓ toggle) and regenerates the scaled printable copy. Print it flat as delivered (it sits on its 0.5 mm break-away plate); snap details at ×0.78 want a well-tuned printer — test one before the party.
- **`encore_chain.stl`** (`python pendant.py --chain`) — parametric fallback chain: print-in-place closed loop of 94 slim rings (Ø10, Ø2.3 wire, ±38°), 640 mm; lighter but more fragile — use the Cuban chain for the demo.
- `python pendant.py --portrait` → **`portrait_scale.png`** — human-scale wearing view: 164 cm figure (INSEE average French adult woman; man 177 cm), pendant hanging 33 cm below the chin at mid-chest (ideal mic position), chest zoom with true-scale chain + pendant, and a 3D close-up of the chain links.
- `python pendant.py --render` → **`four_views.png`**: 1 closed front · 1b side (thickness + seam) · 2 open shells + components · 3 profile + dimensions.
- `python pendant.py --viewer` → **`viewer.html`**: self-contained interactive 3D (no network needed) — drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, slider/button to **open and assemble** the shells with the exact components. The **🔧 wiring/solder button** hides the shells and shows the battery→XIAO connection with the routed leads, BAT pads, solder joints and step-by-step instructions. `?explode=0` starts open, `?wiring=1` starts in wiring view.

## Component layout (baked into the generator)

**Stacked**: LiPo flat at the back of the pocket, XIAO on foam tape on top, Sense board toward the front, **USB-C edge against the right wall** (1 mm from the slot). Battery leads (JST cut off) exit left, rise into the foam-tape layer and run under the board to the **BAT+/BAT− pads** on the XIAO underside near the USB end — exactly as routed in the wiring view. The FPC WiFi antenna glues flat under the front ceiling's upper band (modeled in the viewer): plastic is RF-transparent, the body-facing back is avoided, ≥5 mm from the touch copper, never against the battery pouch. Click the U.FL plug flat onto the board BEFORE casing it (fragile, ~30 mating cycles), secure with a dab of hot glue, no coax tension when closing.

**The header pins ("feet")**: breadboard headers, not fixings — **clip them flush** (the pocket assumes it); they'd otherwise press into the battery pouch. Solder the three signal wires (touch → GPIO1, LED → GPIO2, 3V3/GND) into the through-holes from the top side. **The camera module unplugs** (lift the FPC latch); only the mic is used.

## Assembly

1. Battery in first, wires left. No JST socket on the XIAO — cut the connector, trim leads to ~20 mm, solder **red → BAT+, black → BAT−** on the underside pads (polarity critical; the on-board charge IC then charges via USB-C). Follow the viewer's wiring view.
2. Foam tape, XIAO on top, USB-C toward the right wall.
3. WS2812B (3V3/GND/DIN→GPIO2) inside the front shell. **Touch: glue copper tape flat on the inner ceiling behind the engraved pulse** (the viewer shows it in orange), solder its lead to **GPIO1 (T1)** — double-tap the pulse = pin, long press = privacy. Sensing works through the ~1.0 mm wall under the engraving.
4. Align the front shell (USB slot over the connector), **press until the perimeter ridge clicks**. Open by prying gently at the seam near the tip. Chain or split ring through the bail.

## Print notes (42 fablab / LabElec)

FDM **or resin**. **Unified orientation (v3.2): BOTH shells print flat on their outer faces, zero supports** — the edge rounds are slope-capped by ≤60° chamfers (`CHAMFER_DEG`, expert-reviewed) on both sides, cavities open upward, and both visible faces get the same bed-side finish (matching halves once snapped). The engraving prints against the bed = sharpest possible detail. 0.2 mm layers, 2–3 perimeters. Resin: as-is. **Print a test fit first** and tune `SLIP` / `RIDGE` — snap tolerance is printer-dependent.
